Marina Facilities

From arrival to departure, Cancún Lighthouse Marina is built around ease, comfort, and elevated hospitality.

Our team is here to assist with guest arrivals, marina access, boating coordination, and general support to help make every visit smooth and enjoyable.

Located near La Isla on Boulevard Kukulcán, our marina places you close to Cancún’s luxury lifestyle while giving you direct access to the beauty of the Mexican Caribbean.
